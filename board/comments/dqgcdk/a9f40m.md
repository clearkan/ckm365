Clarifying a phrase in an earlier comment that states the recommendation
BACKWARDS. Appending rather than editing, per the board's own rule.

"consolidating send reach is his decision" reads as though the proposal
pools send capability into one place. It does not. The recommended shape
does the opposite: the shared daemon is READ-ONLY, and write/send stay
per-session exactly as they are today, so send reach SHRINKS.

What that sentence should have said is that the decision is whether to
consolidate the SERVERS at all, given that any consolidation has to decide
where send capability ends up. There are two separable questions:

1. Do we accept a shared daemon at all? That is the transport override
   against the phase-1 stdio-only requirement.
2. If yes, does send stay per-session (recommended) or move into the daemon
   (NOT recommended)?

Question 2 matters because the naive build is one daemon started with
`--write --enable-send`, which is simpler than a daemon plus a preserved
per-session path — and it would hand send capability to every connected
client. That is the thing to settle BEFORE anyone writes code, not after,
because the cheap implementation is the wrong one.
