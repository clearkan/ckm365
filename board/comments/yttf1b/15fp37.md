MEASURED, both tenants, 10 real messages: a distinctive word from each message's own preview was found in the .eml 7/10 and in the .md record 10/10, with the .md 4-7x smaller (1.8-40 KB vs 8.6-289 KB) — that ratio is the whole argument for the format. Shares _write_target/_write_atomic with CKM-32, so the root confinement, no-overwrite and no-residue rules are one implementation.
